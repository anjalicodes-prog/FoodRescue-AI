🌍 Our Impact

FoodRescue AI helps reduce food waste by predicting surplus food before it becomes waste. The system then calculates an urgency level and recommends a suitable receiver based on capacity and estimated distance.

This creates a simple rescue pipeline: Predict → Prioritize → Match → Rescue → Measure

Every rescued surplus meal represents food that can potentially reach people instead of being wasted.
